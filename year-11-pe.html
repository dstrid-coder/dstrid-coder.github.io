Year 11 Physical Education

Your central hub for Year 11 Physical Education (PED1 & PED2). Access course outlines, assessment booklets, learning slides, and core resources for AS92018 (1.3) and AS92019 (1.4).

Course Overview & Driving Question

Driving Question: How do te ao Māori, biophysical principles, and sociocultural factors influence the way we move, and how does movement influence our hauora?

Learners experience movement in authentic contexts (such as golf), applying anatomy, biomechanics, Fonofale/Te Whare Tapa Whā health models, and socio-cultural factors to optimize movement and well-being.

NCEA Level 1 Achievement Standards

AS92018 · 1.3 · 5 Credits · External

Demonstrate understanding of the influence of personal movement experiences on hauora

Participate in ongoing personal movement experiences (e.g., learning golf) and build a reflective portfolio using the Fonofale Health Model to analyze impacts on physical, mental, spiritual, cultural, and family well-being.

Key Learning Units & Concepts

1. Anatomy & Biomechanics

  • Anatomical Structures: Skeletal system (bones, joints, ligaments) and muscular system (superficial muscles, tendons, antagonistic pairs).
  • Joint Movements: Flexion, Extension, Abduction, Adduction, Dorsiflexion, Plantar Flexion.
  • Balance & Stability: Centre of Gravity (COG), Base of Support (BOS), and Line of Gravity (LOG).
  • Force Summation: 4 Keys — More muscles, correct sequencing, correct timing, greater range of motion.
  • Newton's Laws of Motion: Law of Inertia, $F = ma$, and Action/Reaction applied to the golf swing.

2. Te Ao Māori & Sociocultural Factors

  • Te Ao Māori Concepts:
    • Tuākana-Tēina: Peer mentoring and supportive learning relationships.
    • Kaitiakitanga: Guardianship of equipment, school fields, and course mats.
    • Tapu: Sacred boundaries, safety lines, quietness during swings, and etiquette.
  • Sociocultural Factors: Cost/gear barriers, geographic location/access, and community stereotypes.
