Assessment Overview

Demonstrate quality performance in Pickleball across applied match-play sessions.

Core Skill Dimensions Assessed

  • Offensive Skills: Shot placement, dinking, third shot drops/drives, shot choice.
  • Movement Off the Ball: Spatial awareness, positioning at the kitchen line, footwork, transition play.
  • Controlling Play: Pace control, closing down angles, anticipation.
  • Game Play & Strategy: Partner communication, doubles court coverage (stacking), tactical execution under pressure.
