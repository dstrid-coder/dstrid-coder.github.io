The booklet

Learning objectives: take action to set goals and participate in activities to create a programme to improve your own Taha Tinana; build knowledge of health hygiene practices, physical responses to activity, and the benefits of regular activity to hauora.

Success criteria: you've engaged positively with others and completed both the practical and theory learning.

How you're assessed, in short:

  • No Evidence: didn't come prepared or engage / engaged very little in practical and theory learning
  • Emerging: comes prepared and engaged well; can identify what actions to implement by setting goals to improve their own Taha Tinana
  • Effective: as above, plus can describe using examples how their Taha Tinana is impacted by implementing changes to achieve their goal
  • Exemplary: engaged exceptionally well; can also evaluate by making future changes and expectations

Structure & guide

Task 1 — Define the vocabulary

For Taha Tinana, hygiene, and healthy: write a definition in your own words, draw an image, and give an example.

Task 2 — Deconstructing a healthy person

Around the outline avatar, brainstorm everything that makes up a healthy person — movement, fuel, sleep, mindset, and connection.

Task 3 — The hygiene practices table

For body odour, teeth, physical activity during the school day, feet, coughing, periods, face & skin, and washing hands: identify why each matters for your Taha Tinana, and how it demonstrates manaakitanga to others.

Task 4 — Physiological chain reactions

For heart rate, temperature, breathing rate, blood pressure, and hormonal response: describe what happens, why it happens, and the benefit to your body.

Task 5 — Player stats and your action plan

Take part in physical challenges to record your pre-results, then identify three areas you want to improve, set your target post-results, and design the actions you'll take to bridge the gap.

Resources

  • Physiological responses to track: heart rate, temperature, breathing rate, blood pressure, hormonal (endorphins & adrenaline)
  • Player stats to measure: resting heart rate, working heart rate, flexibility, cardiovascular, muscular endurance, co-ordination, explosive power
  • Gear needed every lesson: chromebook & pen (theory), PE uniform (practical)
