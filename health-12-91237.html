2.3 Health Promotion Action

AS91237 v2 · 5 credits · Internal · Term 3

Take action to enhance an aspect of people's well-being within the school or wider community — planning, implementing, and evaluating a collective health action.

Assessment

The full assessment booklet, including the marking schedule, will be updated prior to Term 3.

  • Achieved

    Plan, implement, and evaluate a health promotion action aimed at improving well-being in the school or wider community using collective action principles.

  • Merit

    Plan, implement, and evaluate in depth by explaining how the action directly addresses identified community needs and enhances well-being dimensions.

  • Excellence

    Plan, implement, and evaluate comprehensively by critically examining the effectiveness of the collective action and its long-term sustainability on community hauora.

Structure & Guide

Work through the steps in order to carry out your community health action.

  1. Step 1: Well-being Need Identification — Identify a specific health issue/need within Alfriston College or the local community environment.
  2. Step 2: Action Plan Development — Formulate a detailed step-by-step health promotion plan incorporating SMART goals and collective action principles.
  3. Step 3: Implementation — Execute the planned health promotion action within the target school or local community setting.
  4. Step 4: Evaluation & Reflection — Critically reflect on the outcomes, well-being impacts, and recommendations for future health initiatives.
