2.7 Are We Keeping Ourselves Safe?

AS91333 v2 · 3 credits · Internal · Term 3

Analyse the application of risk management strategies to a challenging outdoor activity — white water rafting and high ropes at Wero.

Assessment

The full assessment booklet, including the marking schedule, is below.

Open assessment booklet (PDF)
  • Achieved

    Applies risk management strategies during the activity, considers people/equipment/environmental factors, and gives reasons for the strategies chosen, with examples.

  • Merit

    As above, but reasons are clearly relevant to the specific risk analysed and the report goes in-depth.

  • Excellence

    Analyses in-depth, explaining the effect of the risk management strategies on self and others, fully supported by examples.

Structure & Guide

Work through the tasks in order — each one builds on the last.

  1. Task 1: Pre-activity site assessment — before the trip, answer the site-assessment questions in detail for both the high ropes (Wero Climb) and white water rafting activities.
  2. Task 2: Take part & stay safe — participate in the trip, demonstrating safe behaviour throughout (this is validated by your teacher, not directly assessed).
  3. Task 3: Analyse & report — write up how risk management strategies (RAMS, safety action plans/SOPs, and physical/emotional/cultural safety) were applied, considering people, equipment and environmental factors, and support every point with examples from the trip.
