The Exam — AS91462

AS91462: analyse an international health issue. This is the one Level 3 Health standard assessed by exam rather than internal coursework — you'll be given a resource booklet on the day covering an international health issue, and write your analysis based on it.

The assessment Structure & guide Resources

The assessment

Standard: AS91462 — Analyse an international health issue (Health 3.2)

Credits: 5  |  Level: 3  |  Assessment: External (exam)

On exam day, you'll be given a resource booklet about a specific international health issue you haven't seen before. Your job is to analyse it using the same skills as 3.1, just applied to an international context:

  • identify and explain at least two determinants of health contributing to the issue
  • explain short-term and long-term implications for individuals and society, using evidence from the booklet
  • recommend strategies that address those determinants and would lead to more equitable health outcomes

Achieved / Merit / Excellence, in short:

  • Achieved: analyse the issue — identify determinants, describe short and long-term effects, use some evidence from the booklet.
  • Merit: analyse in depth — show strong links between determinants and the issue, explain implications in more detail, suggest strategies that clearly address the determinants.
  • Excellence: analyse perceptively — connect everything back to the underlying health concepts (hauora, socio-ecological perspective, health promotion, attitudes and values), with coherent, well-integrated evidence throughout.

Structure & guide

Read the resource booklet first

Don't start writing straight away. Skim the whole booklet first to understand the issue, then go back through and note down anything that looks like evidence of a determinant, an impact, or a possible strategy — you'll need to reference this evidence in your answer.

Identify your determinants

Pick at least two determinants (cultural, political, economic, and/or social) and explain, with evidence from the booklet, how each one contributes to the health issue for the population involved.

Explain the implications

Work through short-term and long-term impacts, and how they affect both individuals and wider society. Use evidence from the booklet to back up each point.

Recommend strategies

For each strategy, explain what it involves, how it connects back to the determinant it's addressing, and how/why it would bring about more equitable health outcomes.

For Excellence: link to underlying concepts

Throughout your answer, connect what you're saying back to hauora, socio-ecological perspective, health promotion, and attitudes and values — don't leave this for the end, weave it in as you go.
