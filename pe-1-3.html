AS92018 (1.3) Personal Movement & Hauora

Demonstrate understanding of the influence of personal movement experiences on hauora using the Fonofale Health Model.

Standard Overview

In this standard, you will participate in repeated personal movement experiences (learning golf) and build a reflective logbook analyzing how movement influences your hauora across the four dimensions of Fonofale: Physical (Fa'aletino), Spiritual (Fa'aleagaga), Mental (Mafaufau), and Other (Isimea) alongside Family (Aiga) and Culture.
