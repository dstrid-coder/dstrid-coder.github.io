2.2 My Resilient Role Model

AS91236 v2 · 5 credits · Internal · Term 1

Evaluate factors that influence people's ability to manage change — using a resilient role model of your choice as the context.

  • Achieved

    Explains relevant personal, interpersonal, and societal risk and protective factors and describes personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies with reasons.

  • Merit

    Explains in depth by clearly linking risk and protective factors directly to the change situation and showing how strategies build resilience.

  • Excellence

    Evaluates comprehensively by explaining critical interrelationships between factors and strategies and showing how major life changes link to determinants of health.

Structure & Guide

Work through the tasks in order — each one builds on the last.

  1. Task 1: Role Model & Change Selection — Select a resilient role model who has faced a significant life change and submit your choice to your Learning Leader for approval.
  2. Task 2: Risk & Protective Factors — Detail personal, interpersonal, and societal risk factors that hindered coping and protective factors that enabled resilience.
  3. Task 3: Hauora & Determinants — Explain how each factor impacted resilience, connecting directly to hauora/well-being and the social determinants of health.
  4. Task 4: Tri-Level Strategies — Develop interconnected personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies to manage current and future life changes.
