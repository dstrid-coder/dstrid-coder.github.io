Assessment Overview

Critically evaluate personal physical activity experiences to date (Part A) and devise strategies for lifelong well-being beyond school (Part B).

Task Structure

Part A: Evaluating Physical Activity Experiences (500–750 words)

  • Introduction: Influences on physical activity.
  • Life Phases: 0–3 years, 4–10 years, 11–17 years, and Current Participation.
  • Critical Lens: Socio-Ecological Perspective (Personal, Interpersonal, Societal / SPEEECH factors), Hauora dimensions, and questioning assumptions (gender, stereotypes, body image).

Part B: Devising Strategies for Lifelong Well-being (500–750 words)

  • Post-school physical activity needs (tertiary, workforce, travel).
  • Analysis of personal Barriers and Enablers.
  • Action plan and strategies to overcome anticipated barriers.
