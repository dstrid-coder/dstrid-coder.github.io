The booklet

Learning objectives: build knowledge of Hellison's model of social responsibility and, using a reflective process, explain how your own level has an impact on others.

Success criteria: you've engaged positively with others and completed both the practical and theory learning.

How you're assessed, in short:

  • NE

    didn't come prepared or engage / engaged very little in practical and theory learning

  • EM

    comes prepared and engaged well; can identify how their level of social responsibility has an impact on others

  • EF

    as above, plus can describe using examples how their level of social responsibility has an impact on others

  • EX

    engaged exceptionally well; can also evaluate by setting goals and future expectations

Structure & guide

  • Task 1 — Define the vocabulary
  • Task 2 — The values circle
  • Task 3 — Hellison's levels
  • Reflection loop — after every activity
Task 1 — Define the vocabulary

For social, responsibility, personal, attitudes & values, and society: write a definition in your own words, draw an image, and give an example.

Task 2 — The values circle

List the top 5 values held by each group in your circle — yourself, your whānau, your friends, and society.

Task 3 — Hellison's levels

Read through each level of Hellison's Model of Social Responsibility and identify examples of what you might hear and see at each one, from Level 0 (Irresponsible) through to Level 5 (Outside of PE — the legacy).

Reflection loop — after every activity

Log: the level you believe you worked at (with evidence of what you said/did), a value that influenced you, the positive or negative impact your actions had on your team, and your goal for next lesson.

Resources

  • Level 0 — Irresponsible: actively destructive (putting someone down, inappropriate language, acting in anger)
  • Level 1 — Respect: passive presence (negative attitude, sits/stands and does nothing)
  • Level 2 — Participation: going through the motions (follows instructions, little effort or communication)
  • Level 3 — Self-Direction: the competitor (fully involved, achieves individual goals — watch the trap of being selfish or overly competitive)
  • Level 4 — Helping Others & Leadership: the playmaker (enthusiastic, communicates and encourages others, shows pride in the team)
  • Level 5 — Outside of PE: the legacy (trying these goals in other areas of life, being a role model, helping others reach this level)
