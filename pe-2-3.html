2.3 Move to Improve

AS91329 v2 · 4 credits · Internal · Term 2

Demonstrate understanding of the application of biophysical principles to training for physical activity — by designing and running a 4-week training programme for the TGTG challenge.

  • Achieved

  • Merit

    Explains in detail how and why biophysical principles were applied, and explains how the principles interrelated.

  • Excellence

    Evaluates how and why biophysical principles were applied and evaluates clear interrelationships between them, supported throughout by own evidence.

Structure & Guide

Work through the tasks in order — each one builds on the last.

  1. Task 1: Plan — set a goal for the TGTG challenge, complete a pre-test, and choose the principles of training (SPORT), methods of training, sports psychology and exercise physiology focus you'll use.
  2. Task 2: Train & log — run the 4-week programme (minimum), logging a workout plan and a reflection for every session, and gathering evidence (photos/screenshots) as you go.
  3. Task 3: Outline the programme — describe your goal, why you chose it, and how you structured the training.
  4. Task 4: Explain the application — explain fully how and why you applied each method/principle/sports psychology technique, with specific examples from your own training.
  5. Task 5: Evaluate — weigh up the strengths and weaknesses of what you used and how they interrelated, and describe what you'd modify next time.
