The assessment

Standard: AS91500 v2 — Evaluate the effectiveness of a performance improvement programme

Credits: 5  |  Level: 3 Achievement Standard

Over the course of the unit, you'll take part in a Softball performance improvement programme and collect evidence — a training log and video — about your skills, ability, knowledge, and tactical awareness before, during, and after the programme.

You'll then evaluate the programme's effectiveness, using biophysical principles (motor learning, functional anatomy, biomechanics, sports psychology) and sociocultural factors to make and support your judgements about what worked, what didn't, and why.

Achieved / Merit / Excellence, in short:

  • A

    evaluate the effectiveness of your programme using biophysical principles and sociocultural factors, supporting your judgements with evidence.

  • M

    evaluate the effectiveness of your programme in depth.

  • E

    critically evaluate the effectiveness of your programme.

Structure & guide

  • Task 1 — Preparatory reflection
  • Task 2 — Training log and evidence gathering
  • Final evaluation
  • Conclusion
Task 1 — Preparatory reflection

Before you start training, reflect on your existing ideas about performance improvement programmes: what are they for, are they relevant to you, have you done one before, and who or what has shaped your thinking (coaches, media, the fitness industry)?

Task 2 — Training log and evidence gathering

Set up a training log to record, for each session: your aims and goals, the drills/modified games/games used, the skills or knowledge developed, relevant motor learning and biomechanical principles, positives and negatives, and anything else that affected the session's effectiveness. Collect video and log evidence at the start (baseline), during, and at the end (post-programme) using a consistent method each time.

Final evaluation

Did you meet your aims each session? Which drills helped most? Did motor learning, biomechanics, functional anatomy, or sports psychology help you improve — why or why not? Revisit your initial ideas and assumptions about performance improvement programmes (gender, commodification, technocentricity, healthism, scientism, body as a project) — who is advantaged/disadvantaged by each, and what are the consequences?

Conclusion

Would you use a performance improvement programme outside of school? If you ran this programme again, what would you modify, and why?

Resources

Sociocultural assumption categories to revisit in your evaluation:

  • Gender — masculinity, femininity, stereotypes, social construction
  • Commodification — of the body, athletes, sport, sexuality
  • Technocentricity — the body viewed as a machine
  • Healthism — health treated as solely an individual responsibility
  • Scientism — science and measurement used to sell products
  • Body as a project — shaped and reconstructed to fit society's ideal image
