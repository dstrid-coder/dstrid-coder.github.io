1.4 Influences on Movement in Aotearoa & Pacific

AS92019 v1 · 5 credits · External · Semester 2

🔍

Demonstrate understanding of influences on movement in Aotearoa New Zealand or the Pacific — using golf biomechanics, sociocultural factors, and Te Ao Māori principles as the context.

Overview Assessment Structure & Guide Resources

Standard Overview

In this standard, you will produce a portfolio analyzing two distinct influences on golf: ONE from Sociocultural Knowledge (cost, location, stereotypes) and ONE from either Biophysical Knowledge (anatomy, joint movements, force summation, Newton's Laws) OR Te Ao Māori (Kaitiakitanga, Tapu, Tuākana-Tēina).
