NCEA exemplars

These are NZQA's official annotated exemplars — real (anonymised) student work at each grade boundary, with commentary explaining exactly why it earned that grade. They're one of the best ways to understand what markers are actually looking for.

These link straight to NZQA's official website rather than copies hosted here — that way you're always seeing NZQA's current version, and nothing here risks going out of date or breaching their copyright.
3.1 — AS91461 exemplars

Covers grade boundaries from Low Excellence down to High Not Achieved, using real student answers on alcohol misuse and teenage pregnancy/parenting as the health issues.
