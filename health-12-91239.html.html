Grade Ladder Summary

  • Achieved: Explain influences on gender and sexual identity. Recommend a relevant combination of personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies reflecting social justice values using evidence from resources.
  • Merit: Explain in depth why or how these influences impact gender and sexual identity using supporting evidence, and clearly explain how strategies reflect social justice values.
  • Excellence: Analyse comprehensively by explaining interrelationships between personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies (how individual actions feed into societal strategies) and engage critically with evidence.

Task Breakdown & Sequence

  1. Task 1 - Media Portrayals (~1000 words): Screenshot and describe images of women in music videos and men in sitcoms. Discuss underlying media messages and gender assumptions.
  2. Task 2 - Pornography & Youth (~500 words): Outline statistics on youth pornography consumption, explain how it alters relationship expectations, and analyse impacts on Hauora.
  3. Task 3 - LGBTQIA+ Identity (~500 words): Analyse societal attitudes and norms towards LGBTQIA+ youth using Harry's scenario, statistics, and impacts on hauora.
  4. Task 4 - Social Justice Strategies (~500 words): Define social justice, recommend 2 societal, 2 interpersonal, and 2 personal strategies, and explain their interrelationships for Excellence.
