2.5 Gender and Sexuality

AS91239 v2 · 5 credits · Internal · Term 2

Analyse issues related to sexuality and gender to develop strategies for addressing the issues — structured as a health magazine article.

  • Achieved

    Explains influences on gender and sexual identity and recommends personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies reflecting social justice values.

  • Merit

    Explains in depth why or how these influences impact gender and sexual identity using supporting evidence, and clearly explains how strategies reflect social justice values.

  • Excellence

    Analyses comprehensively by explaining interrelationships between personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies and engaging critically with evidence.

Structure & Guide

Work through the tasks in order — each section forms a part of your magazine article write-up.

  1. Task 1: Media Portrayals (~1000 words) — Screenshot and analyse gender expectations in music videos and sitcoms, uncovering underlying media assumptions.
  2. Task 2: Pornography & Youth (~500 words) — Outline youth pornography consumption statistics, explain altered relationship expectations, and analyse impacts on hauora.
  3. Task 3: LGBTQIA+ Identity (~500 words) — Analyse societal attitudes towards LGBTQIA+ youth using case study scenarios, statistical data, and hauora dimensions.
  4. Task 4: Social Justice Strategies (~500 words) — Recommend 2 societal, 2 interpersonal, and 2 personal strategies aligned with social justice, showing their interrelationships.
