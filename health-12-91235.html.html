Assessment

This standard is externally assessed via a national written examination at the end of Term 4.

  • Achieved

    Analyses an adolescent health issue by explaining factors influencing the issue, short- and long-term consequences on hauora, and strategies at tri-levels.

  • Merit

    Analyses in depth by explaining detailed cause-and-effect connections between influences, impacts across all four dimensions of hauora, and effective strategies.

  • Excellence

    Analyses comprehensively by engaging critically with evidence, explaining interrelationships between factors and strategies, and evaluating broader impacts on NZ society.

Structure & Guide

Work through these revision steps in preparation for the external exam.

  1. Step 1: Context Investigation — Examine key adolescent issues (vaping, energy drinks, period poverty, sleep deprivation).
  2. Step 2: Influencing Factors — Categorise and explain personal, interpersonal, and societal factors driving adolescent health choices.
  3. Step 3: Hauora Impact Mapping — Evaluate short-term and long-term consequences on Taha Tinana, Taha Hinengaro, Taha Whānau, and Taha Wairua.
  4. Step 4: Strategy Design — Formulate interconnected personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies grounded in health promotion and social justice.
