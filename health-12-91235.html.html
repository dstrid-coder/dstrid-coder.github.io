External Examination Overview & Grade Requirements

This standard is assessed via a national external examination at the end of Term 4.

Grade Ladder Summary

  • Achieved: Analyse an adolescent health issue by explaining factors influencing the issue, short and long-term consequences on Hauora, and strategies at personal, interpersonal, and societal levels.
  • Merit: Analyse in depth by explaining detailed cause-and-effect connections between influences, impacts on all four dimensions of Hauora, and effective strategies.
  • Excellence: Analyse comprehensively by engaging critically with evidence, explaining interrelationships between factors and strategies, and evaluating broader impacts on NZ society.

Revision Task & Exam Preparation Sequence

  1. Issue Exploration: Investigate key adolescent issues (e.g., Vaping, Energy Drink consumption, Period Poverty, Sleep deprivation).
  2. Socio-Ecological Factors: Categorise and explain personal, interpersonal, and societal influences contributing to the health issue.
  3. Hauora Impact Mapping: Evaluate short-term and long-term consequences across Taha Tinana, Taha Hinengaro, Taha Whānau, and Taha Wairua.
  4. Multi-Level Strategy Design: Develop interconnected personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies supporting social justice and health promotion.
  5. Mock Exam Practice: Complete timed practice exam essay questions under exam conditions.
