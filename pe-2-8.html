2.8 Being Socially Responsible

AS91334 v2 · 3 credits · Internal · Term 3

Consistently demonstrate social responsibility through applying a social responsibility model in physical activity — using Don Hellison's Model.

  • Merit

    Consistently demonstrates a high level of social responsibility through applying the model.

  • Excellence

    Consistently demonstrates an outstanding level of social responsibility, including in wider contexts beyond the classroom.

Structure & Guide

Work through the tasks in order — each one builds on the last.

  1. Task 1: Learn the model — get familiar with Hellison's five levels of social responsibility, from Level 0 (no social responsibility) through to Level 5 (beyond the classroom).
  2. Task 2: Take part & reflect — across a minimum of 6 physical activity sessions, identify the level you demonstrated, what deliberate actions you took to improve, and how your behaviour affected others.
  3. Task 3: Seek feedback — get feedback and feedforward from peers as critical friends, and use it in your next reflection.
  4. Task 4: Show growth over time — your later reflections should show clear, deliberate progress toward consistently demonstrating a high level of social responsibility.
