Year 9 HPE

Your central hub for Year 9 Health and Physical Education units, worksheets, and resources.

Our driving question this year: how can we become the most healthy versions of ourselves? Across four 10-week units we'll look at what influences our participation and movement, and build strategies to improve hauora for ourselves and others — through hands-on activities, reflection, and real experience rather than sitting still in a classroom.

Our four units this year

  • Hauora 10 weeks
  • Health Promotion 10 weeks
  • Socio-Ecological Perspective 10 weeks
  • Attitudes & Values 10 weeks
What you'll need: Alfriston College PE uniform, a pen, and a chromebook. A phone is not an adequate learning device.
