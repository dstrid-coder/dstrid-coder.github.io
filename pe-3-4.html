The assessment

Standard: AS91501 v2 — Demonstrate quality performance of a physical activity in an applied setting

Credits: 4  |  Level: 3 Achievement Standard

You'll prepare for and take part in a series of Pickleball games during lesson time. Pickleball is a tactical game requiring a high level of skill and strategy. Your Learning Leader will observe and gather evidence on your performance across the whole unit, including the games themselves. You'll be assessed on the quality and consistency of your performance across:

  • Offensive skills
  • Movement off the ball
  • Controlling play
  • Game play

Your use of these skills needs to be consistent, effective, and accomplished.

Achieved / Merit / Excellence, in short:

  • M

    demonstrate a high quality performance of a physical activity in an applied setting.

Offensive skills

Your Learning Leader is looking for consistent, effective, and accomplished offensive play across your games — shot selection, placement, and attacking opportunities.

Movement off the ball

How you position and move around the court between shots — getting back to the kitchen line, covering space, and staying ready for the next play.

Controlling play

Directing the pace and direction of a rally — using soft dinks and drop shots to control the kitchen line, and drives when you need to change the tempo.

Game play

Bringing your skills together in real match situations — decision-making, consistency, and applying strategy under pressure across your six-plus games.

Core skill dimensions

The underhand serve

Hit with an upward arc, paddle contact below the waist. Aim for direct, diagonal placement into the opponent's service area, clearing the kitchen.

Kitchen & dinking

Soft touch shots dropped into the 7ft Non-Volley Zone (the “kitchen”). Controlling the kitchen line is critical for dominant net play.

3rd shot drop & drives

Transitioning from baseline to net using soft drop shots or deep, paced drives to reset control of the rally.

Essential rules & faults

Two-bounce rule & scoring

The serve must bounce on the receiver's side, and the return must bounce on the server's side, before volleys are legal. Played to 11 points, win by 2; in standard side-out scoring, only the serving team can score.

Kitchen violations & faults

You can't volley a ball while standing on or inside the 7ft kitchen line, or touch the line during your follow-through. Other faults: the ball landing out of bounds, failing to clear the net, or stepping into the kitchen during a volley.
