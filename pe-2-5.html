2.5 What makes a Tough Guy or Tough Gal?

AS91331 v2 · 4 credits · Internal · Term 1

Examine the significance for self, others and society of a sporting event, physical activity or festival — using the 2026 Tough Guy Tough Gal challenge as the context.

  • Achieved

    Explains how and why the chosen event is significant, with reference to relevant SPEEECH factors.

  • Merit

    Explains the significance in-depth and shows how SPEEECH factors are relevant to self, others and society, backed by evidence.

  • Excellence

    Explains the significance critically — identifies assumptions, who's advantaged/disadvantaged, weighs pros and cons, and explains how SPEEECH factors interrelate, fully supported by evidence.

Structure & Guide

Work through the tasks in order — each one builds on the last.

  1. Task 1: Investigating — identify a positive and a negative issue linked to the TGTG challenge and research each of the seven SPEEECH factors (Societal, Political, Economic, Environmental, Ethical, Cultural, Historical).
  2. Task 2: Identifying — work through the SPEEECH table for Self, Others and Society, then identify assumptions, who is advantaged/disadvantaged, and short- and long-term pros and cons.
  3. Task 3: Report writing — write up the significance of the TGTG challenge for self, others and society in full, referencing the SPEEECH factors and supporting every point with evidence (quotes, statistics, media articles).
