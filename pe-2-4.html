Structure & Guide

Work through the tasks in order — each one builds on the last.

  1. Task 1: Take part — complete the TGTG obstacle course/run in a genuine, competitive event setting.
  2. Task 2: Be observed — your Learning Leader watches your performance directly on the day.
  3. Task 3: Get timed — your official time is recorded digitally by the event and graded against the Hamilton time standards for your gender.
