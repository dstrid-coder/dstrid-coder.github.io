Grade Ladder Summary

  • Achieved: Plan, implement, and evaluate a health promotion action aimed at improving well-being in the school or wider community using collective action principles.
  • Merit: Plan, implement, and evaluate in depth by explaining how the action addresses identified community needs and enhances well-being dimensions.
  • Excellence: Plan, implement, and evaluate comprehensively by critically examining the effectiveness of the collective action and its long-term sustainability on community hauora.

Task Breakdown & Sequence

  1. Community Well-being Need Identification: Identify a target health issue/need within Alfriston College or the local area.
  2. Action Plan Development: Formulate a detailed step-by-step health promotion plan incorporating SMART goals and collective action.
  3. Implementation: Carry out the planned health action within the school or local community environment.
  4. Evaluation & Reflection: Critically reflect on the outcomes, well-being impacts, and improvements for future health initiatives.
