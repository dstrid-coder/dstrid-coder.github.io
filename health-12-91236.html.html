Grade Ladder Summary

  • Achieved: Evaluate factors influencing change management by explaining relevant personal, interpersonal, and societal risk and protective factors and describing how/why they hinder or help coping and resilience. Describe personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies with reasons.
  • Merit: Evaluate in depth by providing detailed explanations that clearly link the risk and protective factors directly to the chosen change situation, along with in-depth explanations of how strategies build resilience.
  • Excellence: Evaluate comprehensively by demonstrating critical understanding. Select factors/strategies critical to resilience now and in the future, explain interrelationships between factors and strategies, and show how a major life change results in interrelated secondary changes linked to determinants of health.

Task Breakdown & Sequence

  1. Role Model & Change Selection: Select a resilient role model (real, famous, or fictional) who has faced a significant life change, and submit your choice to your Learning Leader for approval.
  2. Identify Risk & Protective Factors: Detail personal, interpersonal, and societal risk factors that impaired coping and protective factors that enabled resilience during the change.
  3. Evaluate Impact & Health Determinants: Explain how each factor hindered or helped resilience, making explicit connections to overall hauora/well-being and the social determinants of health.
  4. Formulate Tri-Level Strategies: Develop interconnected personal, interpersonal, and societal strategies to maintain protective factors and mitigate risk factors now and for future life changes.
  5. Final Submission & Review: Collate your report/presentation into your chosen submission format and complete final proofreading against the marking criteria before deadline submission.
