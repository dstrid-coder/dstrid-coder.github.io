1.2 Decision-Making in a Health Situation

AS92009 v3 · 5 credits · Internal · Semester 2

🔍

Demonstrate understanding of decision-making in a health-related situation — analyzing personal, interpersonal, and societal influences, consequences, and impacts on hauora (Te Whare Tapa Whā).

Overview Assessment Practice Scenarios Resources

Standard Overview

Students analyze health dilemmas to understand the decision-making process[cite: 15, 17]. You will evaluate personal, interpersonal, and societal influencing factors, weigh short and long-term consequences on hauora, and justify the best proposed choices using Te Whare Tapa Whā[cite: 12, 13, 14, 17].
