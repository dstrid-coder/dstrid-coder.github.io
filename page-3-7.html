Assessment Overview

Analyse safety management issues based on outdoor activity experiences (e.g., Poor Knights Islands snorkeling) and devise justified safety management strategies.

Report Outline

  • Introduction: Definition of Risk Management & contextual overview.
  • Risk Identification & Analysis: Examine 3 primary risks (Environmental, Cultural, Physical) across pre-event, during-event, and post-event phases.
  • Merit/Excellence: Inter-relationship between risks, sociocultural/ethical implications, challenging safety assumptions, and impacts on self, others, and society.
