1.1 Hauora in a Health-Related Context

AS92008 v1 · 5 credits · Internal · Semester 2

🔍

Demonstrate understanding of hauora in a health-related context through the application of a health model (Te Whare Tapa Whā) — exploring garden planning, sustainable food production, and community well-being.

Overview Planning Resources

Standard Overview

In this internal assessment, students explore how practical activities (such as planning, preparing, and planting in the Alfriston College māara) impact holistic well-being (hauora)[cite: 15, 18]. Using Te Whare Tapa Whā, learners examine food affordability, companion planting, and the interconnectedness of physical, mental, social, and spiritual health[cite: 15, 18].
